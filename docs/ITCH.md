# LAST FLAME

*Everything burns out. Carry the fire anyway.*

You are a spark: a little flame with no face and only seconds to live.

Every flame goes out. A match, a candle, a lamp each burns for a while and then is gone. But fire itself has never gone out. It passes from flame to flame, and they call that the **Will of Fire**. Run, jump and dash across nine levels of floating islands to carry it home to the Eternal Fire.

## Your life is a clock

The number in the middle of the screen is how many seconds of flame you have left, and it is always counting down.

- **Burn lamps to live longer.** Run through a diya for +3 seconds, a candle for +5, or a lantern for +8.
- **Your moves cost you.** A double jump burns a second, and so does a dash.
- **Nothing you stand on lasts.** Paper catches fire under your feet. Wax melts. Glass blinks in and out. Stone crumbles. Water puts you out, drops fall from the dark, wind gusts and rain make you burn faster.

## Nine levels

Kindling · Updraft · Paper Bridges · Wax Garden · Drip · Draft · Monsoon · Ashfall · **Will of Fire**

Each level adds something new and gets harder. Each has one checkpoint: step on the rune circle to light its braziers. Lives are unlimited, so when you go out you start again from your last checkpoint. In the final level, the way behind you falls away as you climb.

## Coming back for more

- **Medals:** each level has gold and silver par times.
- **Ghost:** race a pale copy of your best run.
- **Hidden lanterns:** every level has one off the main path.
- **Assist mode:** your flame burns 30% slower, for anyone who just wants to see the ending.

## Controls

| | Keyboard and mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / arrow keys | Left stick |
| Look | Mouse | Right stick |
| Jump (press again in the air to double jump) | Space | A |
| Dash | Shift | B / X / RB |
| Back to checkpoint | R | Y |
| Pause | Esc | Start |
| Full screen | F | |

Plays in the browser. Best in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a laptop or desktop. Use Settings to lower the graphics on older machines, change mouse sensitivity, or invert the mouse.

## How it fits the theme

**Everything is temporary.** You are temporary: a countdown with legs. The lamps that keep you alive are gone once you burn them. The platforms burn, melt, blink and crumble under you. The only thing that lasts is what you pass on.
