LAST FLAME
Everything burns out. Carry the fire anyway.
Made for BYOG 2026 (theme: Everything is Temporary).


HOW TO PLAY (no install needed)
-------------------------------
1. Unzip this file.
     Windows: right-click the zip > Extract All > Extract.
     Mac: double-click the zip.
2. Open the folder and double-click LastFlame.html.
   It opens in your web browser and the game starts at the title screen.
3. Click PLAY. Click once inside the game to capture the mouse.

If LastFlame.html opens in something other than a browser (a text editor,
for example): right-click it > Open with > Google Chrome (or Microsoft Edge
or Firefox).

Best in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a laptop or desktop with a
keyboard and mouse. A gamepad works too. Turn your sound on.


CONTROLS
--------
  Move ........................ W A S D  or arrow keys
  Look ........................ mouse
  Jump ........................ Space  (press again in the air: double jump)
  Dash ........................ Shift
  Back to checkpoint .......... R
  Pause ....................... Esc
  Full screen ................. F

  Gamepad: left stick move, right stick look, A jump, B/X/RB dash,
  Y back to checkpoint, Start pause.


HOW IT WORKS
------------
The number at the top is how many seconds of flame you have left.
Run through lamps to get more time: a diya gives 3 seconds, a candle 5,
a lantern 8. A double jump or a dash costs 1 second.
Step on the glowing rune circle to set your checkpoint. Lives are
unlimited, so when you go out you start again from there.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- Black screen or very slow: Esc > Settings > Graphics > Low.
  Also make sure your browser's hardware acceleration is on.
- The mouse doesn't turn the camera: click inside the game window once.
- No sound: click anywhere in the game once (browsers keep sound off until
  you do), and check the volume in Settings.
- Your progress is saved in the browser you play in. It stays as long as
  you open the game from the same place in the same browser.
